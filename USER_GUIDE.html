Junkho Studio

Accounting System User Guide & SOP - Sdn Bhd Edition

1. Initial Configuration

1 Set Local Company Details
Open config.js file in your main folder. Update the metrics values with your current Cash Balance. This ensures your dashboard starts from your real bank balance.
Tip: Always double check your ssm and address fields in config.js as they appear on your official reports.

2. Daily Record Keeping (Double-Entry)

1 Standard Expenses
Go to Journal Entry. For every expense, use two lines:
  • Debit: The Expense Account (e.g. Cost of Goods Sold)
  • Credit: Cash in Bank (101)
  • Always select the related Project to track profit margins properly.
2 Incoming Revenue
When a client pays you:
  • Debit: Cash in Bank (MYR In)
  • Credit: Sales Revenue

3. Project Tracking & Margins

1 New Renovations
Before recording an invoice, click Create New Project in the Project List. Assign a Name, Location, and Value. All future journal entries will allow you to "Tag" this project.
The system automatically calculates Net Margin % based on the total expenses tagged to that project.

4. Malaysian Statutory Modules

1 Payroll (EPF/SOCSO/PCB)
Use the Statutory Payroll tab to add Employees or Directors. Enter their Gross Salary, and the system automatically calculates the Malaysian statutory deductions. Click "Confirm" at the end of every month.
2 SST-02 Filing
Every two months, check your SST-02 Filing Module. Type in your Output Tax for the period and click Download SST-02 Form to generate your filing summary ready for LHDN.

5. Bulk Import & Data Sync

1 Transitioning from Excel
If you have 100+ transactions from your previous system, use the Download Sample CSV Template link in the Data Sync tab. Paste your data in and upload it to import your entire history in seconds.

6. Access from Other Laptops

1 Method A: Cloud Sync (Easiest)
Move your entire "Account System for Junkho" folder into a cloud-synced drive like OneDrive, Google Drive, or Dropbox. When you save a record on one laptop, it will automatically update on your other devices.
2 Method B: GitHub Sync
Use git push on your current laptop to save your data to the cloud. On your other laptop, run git pull to fetch the latest records.
Security Note: Your data is currently stored in config.js. Keeping this file synced is the key to seeing your data on multiple laptops.
